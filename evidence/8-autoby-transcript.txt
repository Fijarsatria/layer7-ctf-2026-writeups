## TL;DR

`Forgotten` is a Rust/axum file-sharing service. Reporting a file makes the
service generate a review-only **evidence slip** whose `reviewer-token` field is
actually the challenge flag, then it "destroys" the slip by expiring it.

Two mistakes keep the data alive:

1. Album downloads larger than 8 KiB run a snapshot-maintenance pass that
   writes `maintenance.json` into the ZIP, listing every retired alias,
   including the evidence slip alias.
2. The CDN/nginx in front of the app cached the review fetch of
   `/f/<evidence_alias>`. After restoring that alias with the upload `claim`
   field, the stale cached evidence slip is served again — flag included.
